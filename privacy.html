Privacy

Biscuit Lane · last updated 2 September 2026

Biscuit Lane does not collect anything, does not send anything anywhere, and has no accounts.

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page is only the detail behind it, because a one-line privacy policy is usually hiding something and this one is not.

What the game stores

Your progress — the levels you have cleared, your animals and their names, what you have bought, your settings — is saved on your own device and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes it. There is no backup, no sync and no server that has ever seen it.

What the game sends

Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDK of any kind. The game does not have a server.

Everything you see is drawn and every sound is synthesised as the game runs, so it does not even fetch images, fonts or audio while you play. It works with the phone in flight mode from the first launch.

Purchases

If you buy something in the game, the purchase is handled entirely by your app store — Google Play or the App Store. They tell the game that a purchase succeeded and nothing else. Your name, your card and your billing address never reach us, because there is no “us” for them to reach: no server, no database, no account.

Permissions

  • Vibration — for the small taps you feel when tiles clear. You can switch it off in Settings.
  • Internet — used only so the app store can confirm a purchase. The game itself never requests anything over the network.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, there is nothing to treat differently for a child than for anyone else, and nothing that could be shared with anybody.

Changes

If this ever stops being true — if a version adds anything that leaves your device — this page will say so before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
