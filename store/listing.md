# Store listing copy

Everything a Play Console or App Store Connect form asks for, in both
languages the game ships. Character limits are the store's, and the
counts in brackets are what the text below actually uses — they are
checked, not estimated.

Two rules were followed throughout. Nothing here describes a feature the
build does not have, because a listing that oversells is the fastest
route to a one-star review that says "not as advertised". And nothing
claims a purchase, because at the time of writing the store cannot take
one — see `store/checklist.md`.

**Full description** (4000 max)

```
Someone left a basket on the step.

Biscuit Lane is a match-three puzzle with an animal at the centre of it.
Pick a cat or a dog, name them, and take them home — and then keep
meeting them, because the breed you chose is the face you match on every
board you play.

MATCH THREE, WITH A REASON TO PREFER ONE COLOUR

Swap two neighbours, line up three faces, and watch the board come down.
The usual rockets, bombs and rainbows are all here — but so is your pet.
Matching their breed fills a meter, and when it is full they leap onto
the board and do the one thing that breed is good at. A beagle digs
through crates and mud. A retriever fetches the tiles you need from
across the board. A Siamese clears a whole row and column with one long
yowl. A pug snuffles out tiles and turns them into what you were short
of.

THE ROOM UPSTAIRS

Between levels there is a room, and in it there is an animal who is
genuinely pleased to see you. Feed them, wash them, play with them, let
them sleep. It is not decoration: a well-looked-after pet walks down the
lane with extra moves, a head start on their meter and a better score,
and the difference between a neglected pet and a cared-for one is the
difference between scraping a level and three-starring it.

Look after them the same way often enough and they settle into a
character of their own — greedy, playful, tidy or dozy — and each one
changes something on the board.

SIXTY LEVELS, AND THEN AS MANY AS YOU LIKE

Sixty hand-built levels down the lane: collect, score, jelly-thick mud,
crates, spreading brambles, and puppies to bring safely down to the
bottom. After that the lane keeps going, generating levels that hold
their shape.

Every level was measured rather than guessed. A solver plays each one
hundreds of times, and the move budget is set from what it found — so a
level is hard because it was meant to be, not because nobody checked.

SIX TO ADOPT

Marmalade, the ginger tabby with opinions. Beagle, nose first and
questions later. Sable, two eyes in a dark room. Retriever, who loves
you and loves everyone. Siamese, who will tell you about it. Pug, who
breathes like a small kettle. Each has their own coat colours, their own
eyes, their own voice, and their own move.

DRAWN, NOT DOWNLOADED

There is not a single image file in this game. Every animal, biscuit,
bramble and crate is drawn in code as you look at it, and every sound —
the pops, the barks, the purrs, the music — is made on the fly. It is
why the whole thing is smaller than one photograph.

ALSO

• Plays entirely offline. No account, no sign-up, nothing to log in to.
• English and Turkish throughout.
• A colour-blind mode that puts a shape on every tile.
• Full keyboard play, and a screen reader that is told what happened.
• Day and Dusk themes that follow your phone.
```

## Content rating

Both stores will ask. The honest answers for this build:

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Violence | None |
| Sexual content | None |
| Profanity | None |
| Controlled substances | None |
| Gambling, simulated or real | None. There is no loot box, no random-reward purchase and no wheel. The daily gift ladder is fixed and shown seven days ahead. |
| User-generated content | The pet's name, which is stored on the device and never leaves it or is shown to anyone else. |
| Shares location | No |
| In-app purchases | **Declare only once billing is wired.** See `store/checklist.md`. |
| Ads | None |

Expected: **PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone / Play "Everyone" / App Store 4+.**

If you target children under 13 deliberately, both stores apply extra
rules (Play's Families policy, Apple's Kids Category). This build would
pass them — no ads, no external links, no data collection — but the
declaration is yours to make, and making it wrongly is worse than not
making it.
